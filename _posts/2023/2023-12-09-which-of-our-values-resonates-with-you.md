Odd experience of the day, spotted an ad for a nice role in an exciting company and clicked the "easy apply" button.

Got half way through, and a question popped up: "tell us which of our values most resonates with you". So I read up about their values and on reflection, I can't get behind any of their values. Some, I actively campaign against.

Click cancel. "Do not save application".

Phew, that was close...
