So after moving to Scotland I found myself buying a Canoe. Over time, this has led to me spending more and more time on the water and camping on beautiful Scottish islands. This was never a plan, but just an accidental positive outcome:-)

As part of this newfound interest I became interested in boatbuilding, and particularly traditional boatbuilding. At one point, I decided to sign up for a course with Ben Wilde who runs the [Archipelago Folkschool](https://www.archipelagofolkschool.org/kayak-building) which does a class in building Greenland-style kayaks.
