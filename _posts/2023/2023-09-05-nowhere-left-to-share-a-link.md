So this morning I read my rss feed, and found a great link from Dave Winer. I read the piece and it resonated. My muscle-memory fired up Twitter and I was about to post (in that wonderful, collective thinking-aloud platform) and then I paused, and thought "why bother", "what's the point?" and closed my browser.

But I really wanted to share that link. This probably isn't the place, but where else is there?
