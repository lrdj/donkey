## Ten minutes 

Dear Prime Minister<br>
Your apology is empty<br>
I needed ten minutes<br>
In June 2020<br>

Sleep deprived, in shock<br>
And scared of the unknown<br>
I'd just had a baby<br>
And was forced alone<br>

My husband thrown out<br>
Barely given a minute<br>
Left to pace at home<br>
Unallowed to visit<br>

Not even ten minutes<br>
In that precious first week<br>
With his tiny new daughter<br>
Premature and weak<br>

What I would have given<br>
For someone to stay<br>
For just ten minutes of rest<br>
To keep delirium at bay<br>

Ten minutes could have saved<br>
Me from sinking so far<br>
To a low I didn't know existed<br>
For which my mind still bears scars<br>

I'd become a mother<br>
Yet I needed my own<br>
A hug from her in person<br>
Instead of through a phone<br>

To meet her first grandchild<br>
She'd have settled for ten minutes<br>
Instead of waiting months<br>
To obey restrictions and limits<br>

We did it, Prime Minister<br>
To protect our NHS<br>
Stayed at home to save lives<br>
While you couldn't have cared less<br>

Your ten minutes, Prime Minister<br>
Were yours for the taking<br>
You had more than FaceTime<br>
As our hearts were breaking<br>

Ten minutes for a mother<br>
Sleepless and crying<br>
Ten minutes to hold hands<br>
With those who lay dying<br>

You partied, drank and cheered<br>
While we lost our own<br>
You gave us the rules<br>
From your hypocritical throne<br>

And so, Prime Minister<br>
Happy birthday to you<br>
I hope your ten minutes were worth it<br>
From the nation - fuck you<br>
