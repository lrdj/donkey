## My deck set-up in video
I retained the deck only, opting for trucks (obviously) and wheels from Brakeboard 
* 9-ply bottom mount [cambered pintail deck](https://originalskateboards.com/product/pintail-46-complete/)
* [Modus Riser Pads 1/8" - Blue](https://www.nativeskatestore.co.uk//skateboards-c7/riser-pads-c36/modus-bearings-modus-riser-pads-1-8-blue-p17463/s35948)
* [Bones Hardcore Bushings Hardness: Black Hard](https://www.nativeskatestore.co.uk//skateboards-c7/bushings-c38/bones-hardcore-bushings-p3955/s7495)

## My old deck set-up for comparison purposes
![Original Longboards 46" pintail with artwork by Jay Alders](/images/46-pintail-deck.jpg){: .full}

The deck was purchased as a complete form the Original Skateboards website
* Trucks: Original S10 250mm Trucks
* No truck spacers (250mm truck width and deck camber clear the wheels even at full lock)
* White high-tensions springs
* Wheels: RAD Advantage 74MM 78a (White)
* Bearings: Cell Ceramics
