Managed to finish work on time on Wednesday. Nice thing about living in Scotland is it stays light later in summer. Looking out the window as I write this at almost 9pm the sun is still in the sky.

<a href="/images/Canoe-Forth.jpeg" alt="Canoe pulled up on a shoal in the middle of the Forth just south of the mouth of the Teith" title="view larger image">![Shoal on the Forth](/images/Canoe-Forth-sm.jpeg)</a>

So lockdown is begining to be lifted but big Nicola is saying I need to stay 5 miles for my home.

![Shoal on the Forth](/images/Forth-map.jpg)

The other nice thing about Scotland is you can usually find a body of water fairly close by. The Forth is a bit of a dirty old river, runs through major argicultural land and it's on the murky side. Counting the minutes til I can head back to the lochs out west near Callander. Hopefully soon...
