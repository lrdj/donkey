Back in 2009 I linked to [a fantastic cover of Police Truck](/police-truck/) by a young woman posting Youtube videos of her playing guitar with Dead Kennedys playing in the background.

She went offline for a while but has come back, still making great music, and publishing a video on his Youtube channel explaining the whole journey.

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/XKStSNTUe_Q" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

From his Youtube page

> Hey y'all, I'm back, and I'm finally coming clean about my absence. 
> Long story short, I am transgender and took time away from YouTube 
> to pursue medical treatment for my gender dysphoria. 
> (perhaps not the most surprising thing given the evolution of my videos lol). 
> ANYWAY, moving on now... Tons of music things in the works. Stay tuned.

He's super talended and has a [Patreon account](https://www.patreon.com/kidquiet) for folk wanting to support the music.
