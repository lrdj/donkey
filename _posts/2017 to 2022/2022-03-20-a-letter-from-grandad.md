As you know kids, Grandad Jed goes through life with a sketchpad shoved down the back of his trousers. Over the years his collection of "Jed's Red Books" has grown, and occasionally, he sends a photograph of some of the pages. 

He sent this page just now...

> There are good drawings
> and there are bad drawings
> and, 
> there are sometimes sad drawings.
> Waiting to check-in on Eurostar at Gare du Nord
> the queue lines up on the gallery overlooking the concourse.
>
> I found it strange to be a witness 
> to this tragic group of refugees from Ukraine 
> and to be unable to help.
>
> Just drawing is a form of witness I suppose.
>
> I couldn't help remembering me as a six-year-old boy
> being separated from home on endless crowded trains.
> It is still hard to see all this in the modern Europe of 2022.
>
> We deserve better.
> with Love,
> Jed
>

Thanks for sharing this Jed.
