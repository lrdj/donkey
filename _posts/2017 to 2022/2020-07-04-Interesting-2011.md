OK I finally got my pictures from Interesting downloaded. 

What? It only took 19 years, what do you want from me?!? I mean the attendees are probably decomposing by now and no one is interested. Perfect time to upload my crappy pics:-)

![Mr Reid's fission demo](/images/int-2011/roo-reynolds-interesting2011.jpg)

Worth noting that Roo Reynolds took some [much better pictures](https://www.flickr.com/photos/rooreynolds/albums/72157626995034258) which are believe it or not still on Flickr (smile)

I stole it from Roo, the original is [here](https://www.flickr.com/photos/rooreynolds/5848102172/). Paul Downey shot [a video of the demo](https://flic.kr/p/9UAZpf) with his camera cleverly placed on the floor amongst the ping-pong balls.
