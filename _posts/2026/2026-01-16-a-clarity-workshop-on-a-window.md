Clearing up my laptop and came across this photograph of an A4 piece of paper blutacked to a customer's office window. It's an interesting example of a clarity workshop tool in use.

Before a team (or even an individual) can structure a sound what-should-we-do-next plan, they need to have a good picture of their purpose.

Additionally, it's really not helpful to have a purpose that's expressed in jargon or corp-speak; success depends on everyone in your team being able to answer the question "so why did we come to work today?".

By using a workshop tool like this you write it together, and when we do things together outcomes are almost always improved:-)
