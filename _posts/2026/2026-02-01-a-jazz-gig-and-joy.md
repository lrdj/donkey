So was at a jazz gig last night (haven't done that recently) and came away thinking. The band hadn't played together in eleven years and the performance included three artists who had met that morning.

Sat in the third row, I spent the evening looking at their faces. I can't remember the last time I saw so much joy, everyone was grinning out of control as the tunes bounced between artists (an interesting mix of Asian and Celtic instruments and talent).

I came away reminded of a couple things.

Humans engaging in their passions are beautiful; and humans building something together are amazing (be it music or services). Please build it together:-)
