OK so having thoughts and would normally put them on blog but this is quicker.

What if, #arr was illegal, capped, or regulated?

I just went through 15 years of Apple orders and discovered that over that time period I had spent a six figure sum (starting with a 1) with them. Small mortgage territory.

So I guess that's where their cash mountain came from. Well played Apple...

So here's the odd thought, what if subscriptions were regulated like cigarettes, or maybe credit agreements? What if you were prevented from subscribing to something unless there was a really good reason and/or the numbers added up?
