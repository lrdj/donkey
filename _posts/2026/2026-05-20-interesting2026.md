I kept my phone in my pocket to be present. The year I went camera crazy was 2011, those pics are in [this earlier post](/Interesting-2011/). Also great archive [over here](https://interesting.blot.im).

I've been to many of these over the years and am never quite sure what to say about it. I normally settle on what did it feel like. I forget what year it was but one year someone spoke of their learning about Stan Laurel's Dad as he moved around North Shields. I have a memory of an enveloping feeling as I sunk into her story...

This edition felt more _gentle_ -- it was still full of enthusiasm with all of us following Russ's "always be clapping" rule (not ABC as in Glengarry Glen Ross) but the stories were a little quieter, with fewer explosions and sound effects.

Actually that last bit isn't true. There were sound effects and I needed to leave before the end so if there was a giant pyrotechnic I missed it.

Louise Cato stayed the whole way through and listed the talks on [her LI](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7463009548497260545/)

- Hitler and sea monkeys
- Growing up in care
- Bertolt Brecht
- Punk and book titles 
- The magic of seeds 
- The secret rhythm of the song Videotape by Radiohead
- Stack and Mortar: a magazine of magazines
- 3 things to think about aeroplanes
- Tarot and choices

I think it was Jackie that told the story of growing up in care in the 1960s and 70s. Had a lump in my throat during that one and a slight meltdown after as I chatted with folk. 

Also lovely to see once-a-year familiar faces.

And once again thanks to [Undermanager](https://www.russelldavies.com) for continuing to make these happen:-)
