I needed a picture for a blog post over the weekend so asked Gemini (which uses nano banana). The prompt was: "Please create an abstract image in the style of Paul Klee on the theme of facilitation, using design methods to bring about change etc."

The result was helpful, but I was surprised to see the signature. So we have the Gemini watermark and a forgery of Paul Klee's signature. I'm not 100% sure what to make of this but does this 'attribution' feel a little uncomfortable?
