An eloquent witness from America on the relationship between policy, poverty, and crime.  
“The people who come before the Administrative Law Judges that I work with are not abstractions. They are individuals trying to navigate bureaucratic systems while managing poverty, disability, caregiving responsibilities, and now the fallout from sweeping policy changes that removed their benefits with little warning and few alternatives.”
