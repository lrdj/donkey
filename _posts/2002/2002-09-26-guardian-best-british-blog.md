So the <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/media/2002/sep/26/best-british-blog">full result</a> of the Guardian's Best British Blog competition is up and it turns out the donkey made the shortlist. Not the £1,000, not the £100, but a mention in a national newspaper alongside <a href="http://scaryduck.blogspot.com/">Scary Duck</a>, blogjam, LinkMachineGo and the rest. I'll take that:-)

Simon Waldman's judges reckoned the duck was <q>magnificent - well-written, focused and insightful</q>, which is hard to argue with. Meanwhile, I'm just pleased this whimsical personal/tech blog got a look in.

<em>Added much later: the picture is the Guardian page as it still looks today, Hong Kong duck and all. The original was, of course, a lot uglier. Nice that it's still online.</em>
