Reflecting on how I've spent a fortune on AI product subscriptions over the last six months.

The scenario is always the same:

1. "Create a thing" hook, a promise I can't refuse: sign-up for "free" account  
2\. Realise (usually a few minutes later) that the free account doesn't do what I need  
3\. Watch the (always amazing) intro/training videos to be sure I'll get what I need if I upgrade  
4\. That afternoon, decide to bite the bullet and go pro  
5\. Use the tool to make my stuff. The results go as far as 80% completion (often less) of the task I need completed  
6\. Get really, really frustrated as 80% = 0% because it means I can't use the thing (a car with 3.75 wheels isn’t a car).  
7\. Cancel subscription

These SaaS offers are possibly the first type of product-led-marketing customer journeys (excluding scams obv.) where you do the whole lifecycle (ending with unrecoverable churn) in less than 48 hours.

This makes no sense:-(

Services that ALMOST solve for my needs are pointless. This sucks even more if you're paying for the priviledge.
