What seems like not that long ago I would set the alarm for the middle of the night to hear Steve announce something live. Now, I hear about liquid glass and my gut response is meh...

Not sure what happened there, but it's sort of surprising. Nowadays, what gets me excited is seeing people who never speak to each other come together around a problem space in a workshop, and sharing and collaborating after that...

After all these years that still messes with my viscera:-)
