Going through my notes from last week and finding loads of emails from Fiverr.com. I needed a wee thing done so thought I'd try out the service. Long story short:

- I put more time and effort in than expected  
- The person I worked with was very helpful and engaged  
- I can't use the end result (but left positive review)  
- It cost more than a fiver (yeah, lol)

Not sure what it all means exactly, but I'd be curious to hear if others have used similar services and how they felt about it? Should these platforms even exist? When does micro-task transaction management become some form or assymetrical abuse of power (or does it?).
