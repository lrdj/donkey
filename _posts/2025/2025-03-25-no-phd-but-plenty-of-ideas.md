So I don't have a PhD. In fact, I don't have a Master's either. This can be frustrating, because I get all these crazy ideas and then write them down and get excited and then folk tell me it's such and such concept by someone or other...

So I've been talking to people this week about the impact of LLMs on design and transformation and what does it mean for our processes etc, and the idea that surfaced is that creating a process around the impact of a large language model on a specific interface or flow may be helpful in the shorter term, but the LLM's core ability may render the entire process flow pointless in the medium term.

So I put some thoughts down on a language-based transformation process. Hopefully this isn't a stupid idea:-)
