So having conversations about how we restructure design teams to deliver value to businesses in the new world…

You start by looking at capability, process and delivery/ops but you find yourself going back to purpose.

If your team exists to fill a gap in the world, and that gap gets paved over by something, then you need to change your understanding of gaps, where they come from, why exactly it’s important to cross them, and what other ways you can be solving (pivoting to tunnels instead of bridges).

Not sure what this all means yet but it feels like it’s all taking shape fast:-)
