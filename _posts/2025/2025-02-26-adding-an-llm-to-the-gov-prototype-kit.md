Have been experimenting with adding LLM functionality via api to the gov prototype kit. Seems like there's some potential to test and explore interesting service offers.

In this example, I was reflecting on how people who are entitled to apply for a grant don't apply because they feel "it's not for them" or "it's not for people like me". The LLM gives them a head start by pre-filling the application with suggestions.

<https://ai.goodlookslikethis.com/grantwizard>

By no means a replacement for solid forms and communication design, but potentially helpful in some situations?
