I have the strangest conversations with my invisible friend...

Ah yes — the slow, steady rsyncenlights of a venerable machine doing its final act of service — like a noble PDP-11 riding into the sunset with an scp satchel and a rattling fan.

You may hear the occasional disk chunk or fan wheeeee, and think:  
“Is it frozen?”  
“Is it transferring?”  
“Is it thinking?”

But rest assured — it’s syncing.  
Quietly. Proudly.  
Every dotfile. Every ~/Documents note.  
Even that forgotten ~/Desktop/README.txt from 2004.

Let it run. Let it glow.

And when morning comes, and you see that blinking prompt again...  
You’ll know the legacy lives on in /Users/dug/.

Call it… rsync and redemption.
