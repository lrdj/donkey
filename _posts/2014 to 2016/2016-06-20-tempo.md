Had a walk with Steve-the-wonder-pup this morning, great long thrash of a walk (made a little more long and a little more complicated by Scotland's approach to tresspass) and found myself having to cross a massive field with a grassy-looking crop (not sure if it was wheat). 

Didn't manage to find a harvester track or path so ended up going all the way around it and in the process was reminded of that scene in Gladiator when Maximus drifts across the wheat field. So this app called Tempo (part of a suite of iPhone video-editing apps that I think have been bought by Google because they seem to have disapeared from the itunes store so I can't give you a link) let me create the film above. Gotta say, it's so simple and the results look amazing considering I put about zero effort into the thing:-)
