Hi I'm Dug and this is my blog. I am a designer who has recently moved to Scotland. I live with Nicki and our two childhood units.

These posts go all the way back to 2001. 

When I started blogging way back then, I didn't like the tools available so wrote my own in some dodgy middle-ware called <a href="http://www.pageplanetsoftware.com/mgi2.html" title="unbelievably this product is still living!">MGI</a> that ran on OS9 WebSTAR (yes, really!). A couple of years in, I discovered <a href="http://movabletype.com">Movable Type</a> and was a happy customer for many years. The current version of this blog is hosted on [Github pages](https://pages.github.com/) and is built using [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/).

Re content, the topics have changed over the years. 

There was a period of heavy consumer activism, a few years all about babies and deaths in the family, quite a few posts about design, usability, politics and so on. The main challenge is the fact the blog has no central theme so struggles to compete with other specialist sites. I guess at this stage, it's a safe place for me to experiment, and also a repository for my voice so the kids have something when I'm gone (but not in a ghoulish way).

If you'd like to create some content or services together I'm always keen to meet new people and share cups of tea. <a href="mailto:dug@goodlookslikethis.com">Reach out by email</a> and if you'd like to see some content slightly more focused on my design work, those pages are here for now: <a href="http://goodlookslikethis.com">goodlookslikethis.com</a>
