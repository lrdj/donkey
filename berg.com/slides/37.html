And here’s a third, Newspaper Club, which isn’t launched yet but I know the folks working on it. They get access to spare capacity on printing presses and let anyone print newspapers. This is one they made before, a 1,000 copies for their friends. It’s awesome that it’s not about competing with main-stream media. It’s about taking their printing presses and turning them for any one of two hundred other reasons.

We can all be producers now.
