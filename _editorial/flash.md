# Posts that discuss Flash (the browser plugin)

Flash (a.k.a. FutureSplash, ActionScript, `.swf`, sIFR) — as opposed to
the many posts that merely embed a Flash player (SlideShare, Vimeo,
YouTube/Google Video, Brightcove, etc.). Those embed-only posts are
listed separately at the bottom for reference.

Permalink structure is `/:title/`, so live URLs are
`https://donkeyontheedge.com/<slug>/`.
